## Opportunity for Undergraduate RA

Our lab is **not** currently recruiting undergraduate research assistants.

Research Assistants (RAs) will conduct psychophysical experiments to collect data for ongoing research. Responsibilities include conducting literature reviews, experiment programming, data collection, and preliminary data analysis. Ideal candidates should possess a foundational knowledge of MATLAB, Python, or R, a basic understanding of statistics, and—most importantly—a passion for research, a deep curiosity about the human mind, and an active, proactive personality.
