## PI: Jongsoo Baek, Ph.D

### Bio

Jongsoo Baek is an Associate Professor in the Department of Philosophy at Kyung Hee University, bringing a rigorous scientific background to the intersection of cognitive science and philosophy. He earned his B.A., M.A., and Ph.D. in Psychology from Yonsei University and the University of Southern California (USC), specializing in cognitive psychology, brain and cognitive science, and visual psychophysics.

Prior to joining Kyung Hee University, he held research professor positions at Yonsei University's Institute of Convergence Technology and the Center for Systems and Translational Brain Sciences, as well as serving as a postdoctoral researcher at The Ohio State University. His primary research investigates visual perception, attention, and computational modeling, with a strong focus on developing innovative Bayesian adaptive methods — such as the quick contrast sensitivity function and partial-report procedures — to measure psychometric functions and visual performance. He also explores ensemble perception, spatial cueing, and cognitive-emotional interactions, including emotional memory trade-offs and perception in social anxiety. Actively funded by the National Research Foundation of Korea, his interdisciplinary work seamlessly bridges empirical cognitive neuroscience, computational methodology, and theoretical inquiry.
